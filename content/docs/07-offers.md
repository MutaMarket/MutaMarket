# Offers

An offer is a private message thread between you and the owner of a module
listed on MutaMarket. It is how you haggle.

There is no accept or reject button. You agree a price in the thread and
settle it in game, normally with a contract. Offers only exist for modules
listed here directly. Anything on an in-game contract is bought through
that contract instead.

## Making one

Find a directly listed module on the [market](/modules). Its price is
either the seller's asking price or "Make offer" if they have not set one.
Click it, write your message, send.

Say the number. Put it near the top and write it so it cannot be misread,
"1.5b" or "1,500,000,000". If you are offering below asking, say why.
[Appraise the module](/documentation/appraisal) and look at what similar
ones are listed for first, so the number you give is defensible.

One open offer per module. If you already have one, the card says "Go to
offer" instead. You cannot send offers to someone who has blocked you.

## Managing them

[Offers](/offers) has everything you have sent and received, newest
message first, and searchable. Each row shows the module, its estimated
value, who you are talking to, and the last message. Unread ones are
marked, and the nav item carries a count.

Open a thread and the sidebar shows the module, the asking price and the
estimate. Enter sends a message, Shift+Enter starts a new line.

"Leave offer" backs out. The thread disappears for you, and once either
side leaves nobody can send anything more. Use it when you have lost
interest rather than going quiet.

## Blocking

"Block user" in a thread blocks the whole account, every character on it.
They cannot send you offers again, and every offer between you, in both
directions, is left automatically.

There is no way to undo this yourself. If you block someone by mistake you
will have to ask us.

## Notifications

You get one channel, whichever applies.

If you have linked Discord in your [settings](/settings), notifications
arrive as direct messages: new offers straight away, and a reminder if
messages sit unread.

Without Discord, the same notifications go out as in-game mail, to
whichever character you picked under Notifications in your settings.

While you have the site open you also get toasts, and the offers nav item
shows your unread count.
